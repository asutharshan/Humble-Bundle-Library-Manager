## Community maintenance

The project is designed to support additional community maintainers as it develops. Future maintainers can be listed here following community/project agreement.

Maintainers are expected to protect user credentials, avoid access-control circumvention, review contributions, maintain release notes and preserve backwards compatibility where practical.
